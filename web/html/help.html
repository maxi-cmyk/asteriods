How to Play

Co-op: P1 is the cyan diamond, P2 the amber square. Three lives each, no revives.

Your controls

Flight essentials

Move, aim, survive each wave. One shot per press. 2× fire doubles your shots for six seconds.

Laser

Hold 1.5 s to fire; release early to cancel. 5 s cooldown.

Shield & precision

Tap Shield to arm a stored charge. Hold Precision to move slowly.

Finish or quit your run before Practice.
