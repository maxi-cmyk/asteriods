How to Play

Clear each wave, take down the bosses, and keep your lives. In co-op, P1 is the cyan diamond and P2 the amber square: three lives each, no revives.

Your controls

Flight

Move, aim, survive each wave. One shot per press.

Laser

Hold 1.5 s to fire; release early to cancel. 5 s cooldown.

Shield & precision

Tap Shield to arm a stored charge. Hold Precision to move slowly.

Pickups

  • Multishot three-way volley for six seconds.
  • Shield stores one charge that blocks a hit.
  • 2× fire doubles your shots for six seconds.
  • Health a defeated boss leaves one: +1 life, up to five. Mini-bosses drop one half the time.

Finish or quit your run before Practice.
