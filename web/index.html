Flight settings

Change your controls, then press Apply. Closing discards unapplied changes.

Each profile keeps its own input, sound, and display settings. Choose a profile to use its saved controls.

Aim response controls how quickly the ship turns toward your target. Higher movement response makes partial stick deflections stronger; top speed stays the same. Raise the right-stick deadzone to suppress aim drift without changing movement. Lower deadzones detect smaller stick movements. Mouse movement sets aim, not ship speed.

Flight rules

One shot per press. Right-stick aim stays where you leave it. Laser charge cancels when released early; it fires once at full charge.

Shield pickups store one charge. L1 arms it until one hit is absorbed. Precision movement never spends the charge.
