    Controls

    Aim response controls how quickly the ship turns toward your target. Higher movement response makes partial stick deflections stronger; top speed stays the same. Raise the right-stick deadzone to suppress aim drift without changing movement. Lower deadzones detect smaller stick movements. Mouse movement sets aim, not ship speed.

    Keyboard

    Select a key, then press its replacement. Esc cancels. Arrows, Enter and Esc remain available.

    Controller

    Assign abilities below. Hold Shield + Precision for slow movement; tap Shield alone to arm it.

    How to Play

    Your controls

    Change bindings in Settings → Controls.

    Flight essentials

    Move, aim, and survive each wave. Each press fires once; holding fire never repeats. A 2× fire pickup fires two shots per press for six seconds.

    Laser

    Hold for 1.5 seconds to discharge. Release early to cancel. Cooldown: 5 seconds.

    Shield & precision

    Shield pickups store one charge. Tap Shield alone to arm it until one hit is absorbed. Hold Precision for slower movement; on a controller hold Shield + Precision. Precision never spends a shield charge.

    Run statistics

    Accuracy counts hit projectiles out of spawned projectiles. Laser statistics are separate. Results appear before initials entry; personal bests save automatically. Practice stays out of the initials leaderboard.

    Your run is paused. Back returns to Pause; finish or quit it before starting Practice.
